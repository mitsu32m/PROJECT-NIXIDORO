## Independent quantitative contradiction / design gate
TARGET 205V*0.016A = **3.280W continuous** (project requirement, not measured). Zhang's **174V** observation is **31V below** 205V, or **17.82% higher voltage required** relative to 174V. Six illuminated tubes do not imply 16mA continuous, because lamp current and multiplex duty are not quantified. No defensible scale-up of output power from tube count alone.
At 205V, a nominal **200V rated capacitor** is at **102.5%** of its rated voltage (5V above rating); at a 220V ceiling it would be at **110%** (20V above). Even ignoring surge, tolerance, temperature and aging, a 200V-rated output capacitor is **NO-GO** for a 205V nominal design. This is a voltage-rating screening result only, NOT an endorsement of any replacement rating; ripple-current, lifetime, derating and transient requirements remain UNKNOWN.
Zhang's REF/ground problem is a concrete failure mode: layout and feedback reference noise can drive erroneous duty even if static ratio equations pass. It is not a quantitative bound on present design ground impedance or fault behavior.

## Mandatory topology identification checklist before stress equations
Record for each original schematic image: source image URL or version; transformer exact MPN and pin-to-winding table; dot marks; primary/secondary start/end and whether a tap is common to Vin/GND; switch drain/source and current-sense return; rectifier anode/cathode; output capacitor return; FB/REF/GND paths; snubber/clamp and physical PCB return. Independently trace switch ON and OFF current paths and then derive VDS, VRRM, current and leakage energy. Do not infer isolated-flyback formulas from a non-isolated coupled-boost label. An image alone without unambiguous node tracing is insufficient.
For the 205V/16mA qualification additionally require actual magnetics manufacturer data and guaranteed/tolerance worst-case peak-current, frequency, winding losses, core losses, MOSFET SOA/thermal, diode recovery, startup/open-load and independent OVP/discharge.
