## Batch A: Issue #6, 04:00 + 08:01
Source A [precision divider](2026-11-24-0400-JST-HV-002-precision-divider-static-margin-energy-screen.md), [bias and startup](2026-11-24-0801-JST-HV-002-tlv6700-input-bias-startup-primary-gate.md). Manufacturer specified positive-going VIT+ 396–404mV over -40..125C at specified VDD endpoints; INB- input current -15..+15nA at VI=0.1V, **not** at proposed ~0.4V. Startup correct-output delay max 450us after VDD>1.8V, conditional on stated ramp; OUTB high impedance under UVLO, below POR outputs undetermined/high impedance. 18us propagation is nominal at specified 5V/10mV overdrive, not max hard-off.

**Independent exact arithmetic:** assume Vnormal,static,max=214.13495V, ceiling=220V, Rtop/Rbottom=541.5, comparator ±1%, independent resistors ±r. Let a=.99*(1+(1-r)*541.5/(1+r))/(1+541.5), b=1.01*(1+(1+r)*541.5/(1-r))/(1+541.5). Feasible nominal Vn requires 214.13495/a < Vn < 220/b. r=1%: 220.65937<Vn<213.51627V => empty. r=.1%: 216.73016<Vn<217.38737V => mathematical width .65722V. Midpoint Vn=217.058766V gives Vtrip,min=214.459623V and max=219.667443V. For ASSUMED Cmin=1uF, remaining energy before 220V at worst high trip = .5*Cmin*(220²-219.667443²)=73.1073uJ. **ACCEPTED arithmetic only / CONDITIONAL static possibility / BLOCKED design**. This energy is not actual post-trip delivery; ripple and aging unbudgeted. Never call this an approved 217V threshold.

For an assumed pin current into sense node, Vtrip=Vref*(1+Rtop/Rbottom)+I_B*Rtop. At 5.415Mohm, 1nA=5.415mV referred to HV, 15nA=81.225mV; divider current at 217V is 40.0uA. **ACCEPTED arithmetic; CONDITIONAL sensitivity**, because TI's ±15nA test point is VI=.1V rather than .4V. Whether threshold accuracy already includes any input-current effects at its specified conditions requires controlled interpretation; do not automatically double-count. The A report's ~25% consumption of .325V margin is a conditional comparison, not guaranteed error. **CHANGES_REQUESTED**: provide qualified full-temp bias at actual sense voltage or an alternate comparator, resistor MPN working-voltage/TC/aging chain, actual normal dynamic upper bound, Cmin/Cmax, worst tail, independent default-off gate and discharge. **BLOCKED**: fail-safe OVP, particularly during startup/UVLO/return-open/driver stuck-on.
