## New source evidence (retrieved 2026-11-17)
1. **Manufacturer's own high-voltage precedent:** Analog Devices/Maxim design note [15V Input Converter Generates -185V at 50mA](https://www.analog.com/jp/resources/design-notes/15v-input-converter-generates-185v-at-50ma.html). A MAX668-controlled **15V input, -185V/50mA (9.25W output) flyback** with custom SG5 bobbin transformer ~1:6, fast-recovery 600V diode because reverse stress includes 185V + 6×15V + leakage flyback. This proves ADI has published a high-voltage MAX668 flyback application, **not** 5V input / +205V output / 16mA / sealed PETG suitability. Output polarity, transformer, input current and feedback implementation differ. A -185V example cannot be transplanted directly to +205V without redesign. Primary source is an *application design note*, not an operating-temperature guarantee for NIXIDORO.
2. [ADI MAX668/MAX669 Rev3 June 2016 datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX668-MAX669.pdf), pp1–4: flyback is supported; at ROSC=200kΩ ±1%, oscillator 222–278kHz over -40..85°C, max duty 87–93%, current sense 85–115mV. These are stated under the datasheet test conditions; -40°C limits guaranteed by design. No 205V/16mA system guarantee.
3. [ehbtj 2022-06-25](https://ehbtj.com/electronics/redesign-of-boost-converter-for-nixie-tube/) reports MAX668 + TTRN-060S-013-T, 5V to intended 200V, calls circuit a **non-isolated flyback with common GND** and warns of transformer polarity and a corrected pin map; only a single IN-18 lighting test, no proper electrical characterization. This is a more precise classification than the earlier generic 'coupled boost' label. **Exact embedded schematic connections were not independently inspected**; author's term alone is not a verified topology netlist.
4. [Zhang 2023-04-24](https://zhangshuqiao.org/2023-04/%E5%9F%BA%E4%BA%8EMAX668%E7%9A%84%E8%BE%89%E5%85%89%E7%AE%A1%E5%8D%87%E5%8E%8B%E6%96%B9%E6%A1%88/) explicitly calls its design a **coupled-inductor Boost**, mentions TTRN-060S-054 in commercial teardown, TPH1R403NL MOSFET in source reference, 0.01Ω CS and 499kΩ FREQ resistor. Initial 70V output and ~2.5% PWM attributed to REF ground bounce; revised board reportedly 174V driving six IN-14. 174V is below 205V; no 16mA load/thermal guarantee. **Do not assume the Zhang and ehbtj winding networks are equivalent.**

This is **community seller/author claim**, with no confirmed test conditions, continuous load at 205V, primary magnetics or thermal guarantees; useful lead only, not proof.

## New numerical check: HV headroom is an energy-and-delay problem
Targets: 205V nominal, HV5524 recommended 220V maximum per integration and Microchip datasheet. C_HV is **ASSUMED**; current/voltage below are hypothetical injection scenarios, not measured output waveforms. The incremental stored capacitor energy between 205 and 220V is:

`DeltaE = 0.5 * C_HV * (220^2 - 205^2) = 3187.5 * C_HV [J when C in F]`.

At C=0.1µF, ΔE=0.31875mJ; C=1µF, ΔE=3.1875mJ; C=4.7µF, ΔE=14.981mJ. If the HV stage continues supplying **net capacitor charging power** Pnet=3.28W (ASSUMED, NOT actual switching power), time to cross 220V from 205V is respectively 0.0972ms, 0.9718ms, 4.567ms. If Pnet=10W (ASSUMED transient), times are 0.0319ms, 0.3188ms, 1.498ms. These are energy-balance illustrations only; current-dependent loading, control dynamics, parasitics and C effective tolerance can dramatically alter time.

Even more restrictive: Work integration's static feedback corner already reaches **214.13495V** (MAX668 1.22–1.28V full temperature and ±1% divider, no transient). Remaining energy to 220V at C=1µF is `0.5e-6*(220^2-214.13495^2)=1.273e-3 J` (approximately); at 3.28W net this is only ~0.388ms. At C=0.1µF ~38.8µs. These values exclude overshoot caused by switching and controller latency. MAX668 datasheet's 70µs shutdown-delay figure is **not** an independent guaranteed OVP total stop time; cannot use it to certify 220V.

For a nominal 205V feedback setting, the OVP acceptance inequalities are `V_normal_dynamic,max < V_OVP_trip,min` and `V_OVP_trip,max + DeltaV_after_trip,max < 220V`. The second inequality must use worst-case C effective MIN, worst-case energy injected during response and any load-disconnect/open-circuit event. A comparator setpoint alone is insufficient. Moreover, HV5524 220V is a recommended condition, not an absolute safety touch-voltage threshold.

## Adversarial and design consequences
- A physically large output capacitor increases transient-energy headroom but also stores more hazardous energy, changes startup and discharge time, and must be voltage-rated above the worst-case rail; it is not a substitute for independent hardware OVP and hard-off.
- A high-voltage manufacturer's MAX668 application with 15V input and custom magnetics does not qualify the TPS61023 5V power budget, which remains a separate P0 gate.
- The ehbtj **non-isolated flyback** wording and Zhang **coupled Boost** wording are potentially materially different topologies. Need inspect original image/schematic and exact dot polarity, winding connection, diode cathode, and ground return before transferring FET VDS/diode VRRM formulae.
- Source-specific primary transformer data for TTRN-060S-013-T and -054 (Lp, leakage, saturation min over temp, core loss, dielectric, thermal) remain UNKNOWN. Do not order based on community 3.2A claims.
