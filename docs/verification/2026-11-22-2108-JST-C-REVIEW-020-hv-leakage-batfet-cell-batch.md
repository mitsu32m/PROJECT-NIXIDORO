At n=9, Vds=26.725V and Vr=267.25V. **CHANGES_REQUESTED**: source circuit winding dots and diode polarity must be pin-level verified; do not generalize this conditional Vr to other autotransformer connections. 220V is a comparison ceiling, not an allowed operating setpoint or OVP threshold. Derating fraction is assumed, not a normative standard. Leakage/ringing invalidates static component qualification.
