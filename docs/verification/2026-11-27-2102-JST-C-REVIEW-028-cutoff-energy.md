## Independent arithmetic and counterexample
Using Work007 script's stated assumed trip 219.66744265459187V and Cmin=1µF, E=.5*C*(220²−Vtrip²)=73.107319µJ. Independently evaluated .5*CL*(25−9) with CL in µF: 10/22/47µF gives 80/176/376µJ. Dividing headroom by 8 or 12.5 gives 9.138415 or 5.848586µF for assumed 5→3 or 5→0V, all transfer and zero other tail. Equality is zero margin, not acceptable design limit. A counterexample to interpreting the screen as actual overvoltage: if only 10% of the 47µF energy transfers, 37.6µJ is below the assumed budget before other tail; actual fraction unknown. Conversely alternate feed or magnetic energy can exceed budget even with smaller CL. Voltage-dependent capacitors require energy integral of v*Cdiff(v) dv or a conservative bound, not nominal capacitance alone. Do not double-count energy supplied before trip and stored at cutoff.
