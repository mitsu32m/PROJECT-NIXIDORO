## Independent arithmetic and source findings

1. **A TS ideal-B entry calculation — ACCEPTED (ideal model arithmetic only).** With assumed direct NTC R25=10000 ohm, B25/85=3435K, TI ITSMR min/typ/max 36.5/38/39.5 microamp, VHOT_ENTRY 0.105/0.115/0.125V and VCOLD_ENTRY 0.9575/1.0075/1.0575V, independent recomputation gives:
   - cold entry typical **+1.73571 C**, electrical-only min/max **-0.20194 to +3.72130 C**;
   - hot entry typical **+59.51164 C**, electrical-only min/max **+55.57503 to +63.74282 C**;
   - ideal-beta R(10C)=**18.41044 kohm**, VTS(10C,typ)=**0.69960V**; R(45C)=**4.84687 kohm**, VTS(45C,typ)=**0.18418V**.
   Formula `T_C=(1/298.15+ln((V/(I_uA*1e-6))/10000)/3435)^-1-273.15`. These are NOT guaranteed physical cutoff temperatures; R25/B tolerances, actual R/T, wiring, thermal contact and temperature gradients excluded. For a cell rated charge +10..45C, this unmodified direct-NTC scenario would **not** enforce that interval, including at nominal conditions. That charge interval is a *candidate-specific claim*, not a project-wide approved limit.

2. **TI evidence — ACCEPTED with source-location correction (CHANGES_REQUESTED).** TI p.8 electrical table supports threshold/current limits, and §6.3.9 states charging pauses outside hot/cold thresholds. TI p.19 §6.4 **functional-mode table** explicitly shows TS Measurement YES in adapter, NO in battery-only. **A PWR-002B and prior C-REVIEW-005 incorrectly cite “Table 6-2” for this fact**: Table 6-2 on p.19 is actually the **Status Pins State Table** immediately above the functional-mode table. The technical conclusion is unchanged, but future reports/Work should cite **§6.4 functional-mode table, p.19**, not Table 6-2. BQ TS is not battery-only cell-temperature protection. TI also specifies TS/MR button threshold 90mV (table), long press 10s; the nominal hot-entry 115mV is close in voltage but not equivalent. Faults near button detection require state-machine review; do not assume TS short only inhibits charging.

3. **TE STM010 physical dimension conflict — RESOLVED for manufacturer PDF content; CONDITIONAL for installed geometry.** TE web page links a PDF whose *document filename/title* says “5x15mm,” but the **PDF itself**, dated 01/2022, says `Ø5 x 20mm` on pp.1–3, provides head dimension **20±1mm**, and p.4 explicitly lists **GA10K3435STM010 = Ø5 x 20mm, 1m**. Thus B PROTO-001E's unresolved *title vs distributor 5x20* discrepancy is clarified: manufacturer PDF body and MPN ordering table agree with distributor's nominal 5x20 description. The document title is inconsistent; request TE confirmation of revision/part drawing before locking CAD, but **do not perpetuate the claim that manufacturer PDF itself specifies a 15mm head**. TE PDF's operating range is **-35..+105C**, while the product web page lists **-40..+125C**; this environmental-spec conflict remains **CHANGES_REQUESTED/UNKNOWN** pending TE clarification. TE PDF specifies R25 10k±1%, B25/85 3435±1%, approx 15s time constant in unspecified mounting context, and a *nominal* R/T table (10C **17.95kohm**, 45C **4.92kohm**) distinct from the ideal constant-B model (18.410/4.847kohm). Even exact B25/85 does not fix the complete R/T curve. These nominal values further confirm 10/45C cannot be equated to BQ charging inhibit thresholds.

4. **B PROTO-001F first-order thermal-lag sensitivity — ACCEPTED (algebra only), installed qualification BLOCKED.** For assumed first-order lag `delta_T ≈ ramp_rate * tau`: 1C/min × 30s = 0.5C; 3C/min × 60s = 3C. Neither tau nor cell ramp is measured. TE air time constant is not a cell-mounted worst-case guarantee; heat-transfer and attachment/positioning remain unknown. PROTO-001F was added concurrently and its full acceptance contract remains pending separate physical review.
