## Independent results and verdicts
1. **LT8365 ordinary one-inductor boost: ACCEPTED (topology-specific rejection).** Manufacturer LT8365 product page Rev.A specifies 150V integrated switch, 2.8–60V IC VIN. Conventional nonisolated boost SW off-state approximately VOUT+VD, thus at 205V output SW >=205V even with ideal VD=0, exceeding 150V by >=55V before ringing. This does not reject a switched-capacitor multiplier. Manufacturer DC2838A board explicitly demonstrates **9–30V to +250V/10mA, 400kHz**, optional third pump stage; NOT a 5V/205V/16mA reference. ADI primary pages directly checked 2026-11-21: https://www.analog.com/en/products/lt8365.html ; https://www.analog.com/en/resources/evaluation-hardware-and-software/evaluation-boards-kits/dc2838a.html . **CONDITIONAL** further DC2838A topology investigation; **BLOCKED** adoption. Controlled PDF schematic's component-level topology, actual switch-node voltage, capacitor/diode stress and startup were NOT independently traced here.
2. **LT8365 input/power screening: ACCEPTED (arithmetic only).** 205*0.016=3.280W versus 250*0.010=2.500W; ratio 1.312. At assumed 75/80% HV efficiency and 5V, input averages 0.874667/0.820A; at 3V 1.457778/1.366667A; HV-stage losses 1.093333/0.820W. These are not guaranteed or measured. USB 5V*0.5A=2.5W cannot deliver 3.28W even ideally; net battery current may be discharge. **BLOCKED** actual low-VIN current, temperature, battery-assist and USB charge contract.
3. **MAX668 ideal coupled/flyback-like turns ratio: CONDITIONAL (mathematics), hardware BLOCKED.** Derived Nmin=(Vout+Vd)/Vin*(1-Dlim)/Dlim. For Vin=4.75V and effective output 206V, Nmin at Dlim=.87 is 6.48034; at .82 is **9.51990** (Chat A states 9.518, minor arithmetic discrepancy). At N=10, D=206/253.5=0.812623; at N=5, D=206/229.75=0.896627. At N=9.7, D=206/(206+46.075)=**0.817217** (Chat A states 0.81729; correct to 0.81722). Request corrected rounding and verification of guaranteed MAX668 duty spec/ROSC/mode/temp before treating 87% as guaranteed. Controller topology, coupled winding polarity, saturation, peak current, losses, switch leakage spike not verified. ADI MAX668 PDF https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX668-MAX669.pdf was NOT independently parsed in this run.
4. **SYS-002 capacitor-spread inversion: ACCEPTED (symbolic necessary bound), CHANGES_REQUESTED (numeric table).** For k=Cmax/Cmin >=1 and D=T/[Rremaining,max ln(V0,max/Vsafe)], if Cmax<=D then Cmin<=D/k. Tail constraint E<0.5*Cmin*(Vlim²−Vtrip,max²) implies Vtrip,max < sqrt(Vlim²−2kE/D) for positive radicand, necessary but not sufficient. Using exactly the report's rounded D=1.100uF, E=.2253mJ, Vlim=220V, independent recalculation yields ceilings k=1:219.0670V; 1.2:218.8800V; 1.5:**218.5991V** (report 218.597); 2:**218.1301V** (report 218.125); 3:**217.1891V** (report 217.179). Thus three table rows inconsistent with stated rounded inputs. Correct them or disclose alternate unrounded D used. D itself from R=2.1Mohm,T=3s,V0=220,Vsafe=60 is ~1.0996uF, so a different rounding convention also requires disclosure. None of these numerical assumptions is a qualified device bound; no guaranteed tail energy, capacitor effective range, stop time, comparator trip, or touch-safety threshold. **BLOCKED** actual OVP/discharge selection.
