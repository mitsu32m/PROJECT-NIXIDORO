### A 15:55 / 20:00: isolated, fully-reset flyback proxy
**ASSUMED**: Vin=4.75V, Vout=205V, diode drop=1V, N=Ns/Np=10, Lm=40µH nominal ±10%, eta=0.80 constant, D=0.75, f=100kHz or 250kHz. **TARGET** Pout=3.28W. These are NOT verified MAX668 coupled-boost operating conditions.
For fully reset triangular magnetizing current, Ipk=Vin*D/(Lm*f), Pout=eta*Vin²*D²/(2*Lm*f). Independent results: at 100kHz Ipk=0.890625A, Pout=1.269140625W; at 250kHz Pout=0.50765625W. At Lm=36/40/44µH and 100kHz, Pout=1.41015625/1.269140625/1.15376420W. **ACCEPTED (arithmetic/model only).**
Reset: Dreset=N*Vin*D/(Vout+Vd); D_BCM=206/(206+47.5)=0.8126232742. At D=.75, Dreset=.17293689 and idle=.07706311. At D_BCM idle=0, so calling that DCM with positive idle is **REJECTED**.
At fixed Lm=40µH and eta=.8, frequency upper bound f<=eta*Vin²*D²/(2*Lm*Pout): D=.75 => **38.6933kHz**; optimistic BCM D=.81262327 => **45.4247kHz**. BCM bound at Lm=36/40/44µH => **50.4719/45.4247/41.2952kHz**. **ACCEPTED (model only)**, with qualification that these are conditional ceilings, not manufacturer transformer maximum operating frequencies, and eta is assumed. At f=250kHz, DCM proxy cannot deliver 3.28W under these assumptions. **CONDITIONAL** narrow NO-GO; universal rejection of MAX668, Würth or CCM is **REJECTED**.
For the *specified isolated flyback winding polarity only*, ideal diode reverse stress V_R≈Vout+N*Vin_high=257.5V at 5.25V,N=10 and 259.075V at N=10.3, before ringing and Vout overshoot. **ACCEPTED (topology-specific arithmetic only)**; a 250V diode is insufficient in that model, and a 300V diode is not qualified merely by the ideal value. The MOSFET OFF ideal ~4.75+206/10=25.35V is likewise topology-specific. **CHANGES_REQUESTED** for any extrapolation to original coupled-boost rectifier without actual dot/pin and conduction-path tracing.
