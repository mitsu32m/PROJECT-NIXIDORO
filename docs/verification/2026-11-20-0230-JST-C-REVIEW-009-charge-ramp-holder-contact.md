## Claims and independent dispositions
1. **A: adapter-ramp counterexample — CONDITIONAL (valid logical possibility), not proven occurrence.** A VBUS-derived pullup is not *by itself* proof that /CE is high before charging can start. TI Rev.B p7 electrical table specifies CE VIL=0.4V and VIH=1.3V, explicitly under VPULLUP=1.8V; these are specified threshold entries, not a universal high-voltage-ramp timing guarantee. Same table lists VIN_LOWV rising charge-start threshold 3.0–3.15V and VIN_LOWVZ falling charge-stop 2.95–3.1V, but does not in those rows bound the /CE high transition relative to earliest charge current. Therefore A's hypothetical t_CE_SAFE < t_CHG_EARLIEST is a sufficient startup safety condition for the proposed architecture, not established as satisfied or necessarily violated. /CE default pull/bias, leakage, comparator supply/output below UVLO, and actual ramp must be independently bounded. Manufacturer table's VIH/VIL test condition must be retained; extrapolation to 5V VBUS is not accepted.
2. **A: monitor permission sequencing — CONDITIONAL.** t_PERMIT_ASSERT >= t_MON_VALID is necessary only when valid temperature/connection qualification and guaranteed sink-off state are included; timing alone does not protect a shorted sink or /CE-GND short. Actual integrated baseline /CE=GND: no new inhibit hardware exists. **Implementation BLOCKED**. Battery-only TS monitoring and discharge/HV hard-off remain separate, not solved by /CE.
3. **B: contact loss table — ACCEPTED (assumed algebra only).** For 2.2A, R=0.02/0.05/0.10/0.20 ohm: I*R=0.044/0.110/0.220/0.440V, I²R=0.0968/0.242/0.484/0.968W. For 3A and 0.1 ohm: 0.300V and 0.900W. No resistance is a measured/guaranteed value. One bad contact may concentrate the total heat, so a 0.968W total cannot be split into 0.484W/contact without evidence.
4. **B: minimum-SYS inequality — CHANGES_REQUESTED for terminology and model.** `V_SYS,min <= V_cell,terminal - I(R_PCM+R_contacts+R_wire) - V_path,drop` is a possible conservative *upper bound* on minimum SYS under stated nonnegative drops, not a certified guaranteed minimum; if `V_cell,terminal` is measured at the pack's external terminals, PCM/contact drops may already be included and subtracting them again double-counts. Specify cell electrochemical/open-circuit voltage vs loaded cell-tab voltage vs pack-terminal voltage, all path impedances at same current/temperature and BATFET behavior. Define the actual lower-bound proof using worst-case cell voltage, maximum drops, and mode-specific load/current; otherwise label SYS UNKNOWN.
5. **B: USB-only budget — ACCEPTED (ideal energy bound).** 5V*0.5A=2.50W gross input, less than 205V*0.016A=3.28W output. At this assumed limit, USB-only cannot sustain the stated HV output even with 100% efficiency, before logic losses. This does not establish the actual negotiated USB source or operating-mode current.
6. **B: holder selection — BLOCKED.** No manufacturer MPN, controlled fit drawing, EOL contact-resistance limit, current/thermal derating, connector/harness or battery-pack PCM evidence. P2150K/R reported dimensions are not independently checked here. No physical qualification or procurement approval.

## Cross-system / fault implications
Charging may begin during a slow adapter ramp or monitor brownout unless the *complete* charge interruption and default states are guaranteed. CE shorts, sink shorts, open pullup, monitor UVLO, detached NTC, reverse feed and power loss remain unqualified. Contact drop under HV start may compound SYS brownout and PCM recovery oscillations. Independent HV discharge and OVP remain mandatory; neither CE nor MCU blanking discharges stored HV.
