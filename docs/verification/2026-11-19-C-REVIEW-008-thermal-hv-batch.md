## Independent calculations and decisions
1. **THERM loading algebra — ACCEPTED (mathematical only).** For static shunt R_L, e=R/(R+R_L); e<=epsilon implies R_L>=R(1-epsilon)/epsilon. At R10=17,950 ohm and epsilon=1%, R_L>=1,777,050 ohm. For R_L=1Mohm, e=0.017633479 (1.76335%). Ideal-beta first-order temperature-equivalent magnitude e*T²/B at T=283.15K, B=3435K and e=1% is 0.23340°C. Note this first-order expression is accurate only for small e and an ideal NTC; exact mapping uses logarithms and a temperature-dependent sensitivity. Real leakage, powered-off clamps, TS/MR and attachment remain unverified.
2. **Detachment observability — ACCEPTED (logical counterexample).** An electrically intact detached NTC at the same sensor temperature has the same resistance as an attached one, even when the cell temperature differs. Electrical open/short detection alone cannot prove physical coupling.
3. **THERM charge-permit Boolean and pullup inequalities — CONDITIONAL; implementation CHANGES_REQUESTED / BLOCKED.** The proposed permission-only sink with default inhibit is a plausible *open-fault* contract. A shorted sink or /CE-to-GND forces charging enabled, so it is not single-fault safe. More importantly, the proposed `I_SINK >= (V_PU,max - V_SINK,max)/R_PU plus pin bias` is only a current-capability inequality; must validate worst-case sink VOL at that current, all pin clamp currents, VPU during USB presence and MCU-off, and actual VIH/VIL/leakage. Current /CE=GND prevents external inhibit without schematic modification. No /CE component values, pin-level safety or pack temperature limits accepted.
4. **B P2150R comparison — ACCEPTED (arithmetic only); manufacturer rating CONDITIONAL pending independent primary check; pack adoption BLOCKED.** Pout=205*0.016=3.28W; Ibat at 2.5V and 70% efficiency =1.87429A before auxiliary losses. At 3.0V/60% =1.82222A. 5V*0.5A=2.5W gross cannot supply 3.28W output even ideally. Stated max lengths P2150K 75.2mm vs P2150R 74.7mm, and widths 21.5 vs 21.6mm, follow reported assumptions; actual holder fit, tolerances, PCM temperature protection and terminal/harness details unverified.
5. **Older A HV tapped-boost model — CONDITIONAL (ideal circuit algebra); real topology BLOCKED.** For *explicitly hypothetical* series-aiding tapped boost, M=(1+nD)/(1-D), D=(M-1)/(M+n), Vds=(Vout+n Vin)/(n+1). At Vin=4.75V, Vout=205V, n=10, D=0.7930693, Vds=22.9545V, diode ideal VR=252.5V. With L=36uH, f=222kHz, Iout=16mA, Im_avg=0.850526A, delta Im=0.471356A, peak=1.086204A. Derived diode RMS=0.0356201A, output capacitor RMS=0.0318244A, ON interval droop for 1uF=0.057158V. The source report's diode RMS 0.03564A and capacitor RMS 0.03185A are slight rounding differences (~0.06%-0.08%); revise precision if reused. At Cj=30pF, V=252.5V, f=222kHz, 0.5*Cj*V²*f=0.212308W, **energy scale not proven diode heating**. The cited Würth 750315839 is a flyback part and NOT qualified for this tapped topology; controller frequency, actual schematic, leakage, output OVP, heat and winding ratings unproven. No 250V diode rating qualifies even in ideal screen.
