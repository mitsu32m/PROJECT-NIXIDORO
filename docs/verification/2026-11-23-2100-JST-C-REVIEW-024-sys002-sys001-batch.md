## Independent arithmetic and decisions
**A 16:02 passive one-open bound: ACCEPTED (ideal isolated RC model only).** ln(220/60)=1.299282984; R_survivor,max=5/(12e-6*ln(220/60))=320689.2 ohm; nominal max with +5% =305418.3 ohm. Two independent branches each at nominal 300k ±5% give single-survivor t=315000*12e-6*1.299282984=4.91129 s; with both Rmin=285k, at 205V P=2*205²/285000=0.294912 W, at 220V 0.339649 W. Lower-bound worst-corner dissipation at largest allowable nominal = 2*220²/(.95*305418.3)=0.3336 W. This is a bound on *maximum dissipation across tolerances* for the assumed redundancy contract, NOT a lower bound on dissipation at every actual tolerance corner. Resistive continuous power is non-negligible and cannot be ignored in SYS/PWR thermal budget. Real two branches share PCB and HV/GND nodes; common-cause open, short, residual converter drive, Cmax, insulation and derating remain BLOCKED.

**A 20:00 switched-vs-passive: ACCEPTED (arithmetic only), CONDITIONAL (comparative concept), BLOCKED (fail-safe adoption).** At 205V, 0.294912W/eta(0.60,0.75,0.85) = 0.49152/0.39322/0.34696W, equivalent at 5V to 98.30/78.64/69.39mA; at upstream efficiency 0.9 and eta .75, 0.43691W upstream. **B 17:00 USB-only: ACCEPTED (floor-count algebra under its *single 330k-equivalent* assumption), CHANGES_REQUESTED (cross-report architecture consistency).** Combined assumed efficiency 0.675, 205V bus, digit 3mA, neon 0.5mA, Ibleed=205/330k=0.621212mA. USB 450mA nominal ceiling -> Ibus,max=2.25*.675/205*1000=7.40854mA; 500mA -> 8.23171mA. Two digits consume 6mA, leaving neon counts floor((7.40854-6-.62121)/.5)=1 and floor((8.23171-6-.62121)/.5)=3. Zero digits, twelve neons: Ibus=6.62121mA, Psys=2.011W, ideal 450mA source budget remainder 0.239W before charger/control losses. Two digits + twelve neons: Ibus=12.62121mA, Psys=3.83311W; ideal 450mA deficit=1.58311W. 16mA lamp-only plus single bleeder => Psys=5.048W, deficit=2.798W. These are necessary optimistic power inequalities, NOT demonstrated mode feasibility. 450mA input-limit threshold is NOT guaranteed delivered USB-to-SYS current.

**Critical inconsistency requiring Work decision:** B's single 330k equivalent bleeder dissipates 0.12735W at 205V; A's two independent 300k branches have equivalent 150k nominal (142.5k worst heat), not 330k. With A's Rmin 285k per branch, Ibleed=2*205/285k=1.43860mA, i.e. 0.81738mA more than B's model. At the same 450mA optimistic USB bound, two digits leave only floor((7.40854-6-1.43860)/.5) = 0 feasible neons (and even two digits alone exceed the ideal budget by ~0.0301mA). At 500mA, two digits allow floor((8.23171-6-1.43860)/.5)=1 neon. All-on (2 digits + 12 neon) with dual-branch worst-heat bleeder: Ibus=13.43860mA, Psys=4.08135W, 450mA USB deficit=1.83135W. Note resistor Rmin maximizes bleed current/power; for a guaranteed USB-only mode this is the relevant corner, while Rmax is relevant to discharge time. The scenarios must not be merged without explicitly selecting a topology, which is currently NOT authorized.

Additional qualification: 205V*16mA=3.28W is the target but whether 16mA includes lamp-only or all HV loads is unresolved. No new requirement or user interface mode is adopted.
