## Independent findings and dispositions
### A: ideal DCM flyback conditional mathematics — CONDITIONAL, with numerical corrections requested
Assume true isolated single-switch flyback, complete demagnetization, fixed 4.75V input, 205V*16mA=3.28W output, 100kHz, eta=0.80, effective reflected output 206V, turns ratio Ns/Np=10. Energy transfer and triangular current yield
`Ipk = 2 Pout/(eta Vin D)`; `Lm = eta Vin^2 D^2/(2 Pout f)`; `Dreset=Vin D (Ns/Np)/(Vout+Vd)`. Independent arithmetic:
- D=0.75000: **Lm=15.4773uH**, **Ipk=2.30175A**, Dreset=0.172937, idle fraction=0.0770631. Chat A values 15.48uH, 2.302A, 0.17294 are consistent. **ACCEPTED (algebra/arithmetic only)**.
- D=0.81262: Lm=**18.1697uH** and Ipk=**2.12438A**, Dreset=0.187376, idle≈0.0000040. Chat A table says **18.18uH / 2.131A**, inconsistent with its stated rounded D=.81262, eta=.80, Vin=4.75, Pout=3.28. **CHANGES_REQUESTED**: recompute table with explicit full precision and clarify whether values were obtained from alternate assumptions. At D=206/(206+47.5)=0.81262327, D+Dreset=1 exactly; it is BCM boundary, **not a DCM point with reset margin**.
- At D=.81262, eta=.80: 47uH predicts **1.2680W**, 22uH predicts **2.7089W** by stated equation, not Chat A's 1.269/2.711W; small differences, request consistent rounding. These are conditional power screens, not hardware capability.
- No finite safety margin from D=.81262; actual duty limit, efficiency, f, Lm tolerance, leakage and reset voltage determine viability. Current peaks from ideal energy balance do **not** establish guaranteed MAX668 MOSFET/sense current or core saturation. **BLOCKED** topology-specific component adoption.
