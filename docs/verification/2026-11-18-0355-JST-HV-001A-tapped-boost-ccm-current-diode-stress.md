## Exact hypothetical topology and ideal CCM derivation
Assume primary from Vin to switch drain, secondary in series-aiding path from switch node to output rectifier; switch ON: diode reverse-biased and primary sees +Vin; switch OFF: series primary+secondary conduct through rectifier to output. Perfect coupling, no leakage, ideal switch/diode, ideal capacitor, constant output and CCM. Define n=Ns/Np>0, magnetizing current i_m referred to primary; OFF series-winding current = i_m/(n+1), ON primary current = i_m, ON secondary current=0. ON diode anode ideal voltage = -n Vin (with the stated winding polarity); hence **ideal diode VRRM=Vout+n Vin**, excluding spikes and startup. During OFF, switch voltage = Vin/(1-D) = (Vout+n Vin)/(n+1).

Volt-second balance: D Vin=(1-D)(Vout-Vin)/(n+1). Thus D=(M-1)/(M+n), M=Vout/Vin. Output average Iout=(1-D) I_m,avg/(n+1), so **I_m,avg=Iout(n+1)/(1-D)**. Ideal input average Iin=D I_m,avg+(1-D)I_m,avg/(n+1)=Pout/Vin. ON ripple ΔI=Vin D/(Lp f), and the OFF magnetizing ripple is the same amplitude by balance. CCM requires I_m,avg>ΔI/2, or Lp>Vin D/(2 f I_m,avg). These equations are internally consistent at η=100% only. Real η<100%, diode drops, resistance, leakage and mode changes require a solved nonlinear operating point; do not scale ideal currents blindly by 1/η as a validated solution.

## Reproducible numerical screen
All following are **ASSUMED** except project target: Vin=4.75V illustrative input corner, Vout=205V target, Iout=0.016A target, n=10 hypothetical, Lp=36µH (40µH-10% hypothetical), f=222kHz (MAX668 full-temp lower bound at specified ROSC; transformer frequency NOT qualified), η=100% ideal.

- Pout=205V×0.016A=3.28W. Ideal Iin=3.28W/4.75V=0.69053A.
- M=43.1579, D=0.7930693. Ideal VDS,off=22.9545V, diode VRRM=205V+10×4.75V=252.5V (both exclude ringing/overshoot). A 250V rectifier rating already fails the **ideal** 252.5V value; even 300V or 400V is not qualified without transients, derating and actual topology.
- I_m,avg=0.850526A; ΔI=4.75×0.793069/(36µH×222kHz)=0.471356A. I_m,peak=1.08620A, I_m,valley=0.61485A. L_CCM,boundary≈9.975µH; 36µH is ideal CCM for this scenario.
- Primary current RMS (ON full i_m, OFF i_m/(n+1)): sqrt(D×(I_m,avg²+ΔI²/12)+(1-D)×(I_m,avg²+ΔI²/12)/(n+1)²). Secondary current RMS (OFF only): sqrt((1-D)×(I_m,avg²+ΔI²/12))/(n+1).
- For f=200kHz (illustrative, outside stated 222–278kHz controller range at ROSC=200kΩ), I_m,peak=1.11213A, I_m,valley=0.58892A, L_CCM≈11.073µH. At 278kHz I_m,peak≈1.03873A. Frequency and magnetics cannot be selected from this comparison alone.
- At 222kHz, using DCR maxima at 20°C (0.0935Ω primary, 9.24Ω secondary), idealized Pcu = Ipri,rms²×0.0935 + Isec,rms²×9.24. These are *lower-fidelity DC-resistance copper proxies*, not manufacturer guaranteed loss at high frequency; proximity/skin/core loss, winding temperature and topology/pinout remain unknown.
- CS minimum threshold 85mV means Rcs <=0.085V/1.08620A=0.07825Ω for this **ideal** current scenario to avoid premature cycle-limit at the min threshold. This is NOT a component selection; actual higher peak current, comparator propagation, resistor tolerance and MAX threshold 115mV determine overcurrent fault stress. 3.2A TYPICAL transformer Isat cannot bound fault current.
