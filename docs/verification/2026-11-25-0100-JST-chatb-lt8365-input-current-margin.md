## New screen
For a boost-input inductor with triangular CCM ripple, Ipk = Pout/(eta Vin) + deltaI/2 (neglecting auxiliary current). At assumed eta=.75, 205 V * 16 mA = 3.28 W (target bus case). At 3 V Iavg=1.458 A, leaving only 0.084 A peak-to-peak ripple if peak design ceiling=1.5 A. At 5 V Iavg=.875 A, ripple budget 1.25 A. If 16 mA is lamps only and two 285k bleeders are added (assumed), Pout=3.575 W, Iavg=1.589 A at 3 V, already above screening ceiling; at 5 V .953 A, ripple budget 1.093 A. Negative budget means infeasible under this proxy, not a universal device rejection. Actual charge-pump switch current may include extra branch pulses; this necessary condition is not sufficient. At 3 V minimum efficiency for zero ripple is 72.9% bus / 79.4% lamp-only. Actual low-voltage efficiency, temperature and switch waveform UNKNOWN. DC2838A is not qualified at 3–5 V. USB-only 2.5 W cannot meet either target; battery supplement and 5 V preboost losses must be separately accounted. No changes to lamp current or simultaneous modes.
